Cookies & Local Storage Notice

Template version 2026-09-18 · Production operator details are deployment-specific.

Deployment notice. This is an enterprise-ready baseline template, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Production OAuth is intentionally blocked by the application until the deployment operator supplies its legal name/contact information, confirms the policy versions, and enables the legal release gate.

Baseline Storage

The baseline uses browser local storage for settings, local progress, installation identity, and offline profile information; session storage for short-lived application sessions; service-worker caches for offline PWA assets; and an essential short-lived, HttpOnly OAuth state cookie on the authentication backend during Google/GitHub sign-in.

No Advertising Cookies by Default

This baseline does not include advertising cookies, cross-site behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics trackers. If a deployment adds analytics or non-essential cookies, it must update this notice and implement any consent mechanism required by applicable law before enabling them.

Controls

Users can clear site storage through browser settings. Clearing storage can remove offline progress, installation identity, and locally cached assets and may require re-enrollment for authenticated features.
