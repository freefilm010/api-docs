Educational Scope

Planetary Fate Lab is an educational simulation. It intentionally combines observed science, historical records, current public data, simplified models, hypothetical scenarios, and clearly labeled speculative or fictional concepts.

Evidence Labels

Interfaces should identify whether a result is live data, historical, observed, modeled, plausible, speculative, or fictional. Visualizations may exaggerate scale, timing, brightness, sound, or motion for comprehension.

No Operational Reliance

Do not rely on the Service for spacecraft operations, emergency response, navigation, planetary-defense decisions, public-safety warnings, scientific publication without source verification, or other safety-critical activity. Consult the cited authoritative provider and qualified professionals for real-world decisions.

Speculative Technology

Portals, warp concepts, artificial-gravity concepts, exotic propulsion, and similar “Frontier Tech” content must remain visibly separated from established engineering and should not be presented as demonstrated capability unless supported by authoritative evidence.
