Privacy Notice

Template version 2026-09-18 · Production operator details are deployment-specific.

Deployment notice. This is an enterprise-ready baseline template, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Production OAuth is intentionally blocked by the application until the deployment operator supplies its legal name/contact information, confirms the policy versions, and enables the legal release gate.

1. Scope

This Privacy Notice describes the baseline data practices for Planetary Fate Lab authenticated and local experiences. Production deployments must adapt this notice to their actual hosting, analytics, institutional, and jurisdictional practices.

2. Data We Process

  • Account identity: verified email address and provider-specific account identifier when signing in with Google or GitHub.
  • Device enrollment: a randomly generated installation identifier, device/platform label, credential digest, enrollment and last-seen timestamps.
  • Service data: saves, task progress, achievements, multiplayer rooms, roles, teams, and scenario state where cloud features are used.
  • Legal records: policy type/version, acknowledgement type, timestamp, and sign-in source.
  • Operational/security data: ordinary server logs and rate-limit/security events as configured by the operator. The baseline does not require hardware fingerprinting.

3. Google and GitHub Sign-In

Google sign-in requests OpenID identity, email, and basic profile information. It does not request access to Gmail mailbox content. GitHub sign-in requests basic user identity and verified email access; it does not request repository access. The baseline uses provider tokens only to retrieve the identity needed for sign-in and does not persist those provider access tokens.

4. Purposes

Data is used to authenticate users, enforce device/account policy, provide saves and multiplayer, maintain security, remember legal acknowledgements, support the requested educational experience, and operate or troubleshoot the Service.

5. Legal Basis / Institutional Roles

The applicable legal basis depends on jurisdiction and deployment context. Depending on circumstances, processing may be necessary to perform a user agreement, pursue legitimate security/operational interests, comply with law, support an educational institution’s instructions, or rely on consent where consent is legally required. A privacy notice acknowledgement is not treated as blanket consent to all processing.

6. Sharing

Data may be processed by hosting, database, identity, security, and infrastructure providers necessary to operate the Service. Public scientific data requests may be sent to external sources such as NASA/JPL or NOAA. The baseline does not include sale of personal information or advertising-profile integrations.

7. Retention

Session, OAuth-state, and one-time login records are short-lived. Account/device records, saves, multiplayer data, and legal acknowledgement records remain according to the deployment’s retention schedule, contractual requirements, security needs, and applicable law. Production deployments should publish concrete retention periods.

8. Security

The baseline uses HTTPS-oriented deployment, HMAC-SHA-512 credential digests, short-lived sessions, one-time OAuth exchange codes, server-side provider secrets, database access controls, rate limiting, and revocable device enrollment. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Privacy Rights

Depending on location, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or export personal information, and may have rights concerning consent or automated decision-making. The operator must provide an actionable privacy contact and identity-verification process for requests.

10. Children and Students

The baseline is not configured as a child-directed identity service. Deployments serving children or schools must complete applicable child/student privacy analysis, contractual requirements, parental/guardian consent where required, and data-minimization controls before enabling those accounts.

11. International Processing

If users and infrastructure are located in different countries, the operator must document applicable transfer mechanisms and processor terms where required.

12. Changes and Contact

Material privacy changes should receive a new version and be presented before continued authenticated use when required. The production deployment must provide its operator identity and privacy contact.
