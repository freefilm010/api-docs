Community Standard

Multiplayer, shared missions, classroom rooms, creator tools, and future public features are intended for collaborative science learning and simulation.

Required Conduct

  • Treat other participants respectfully; no harassment, threats, hate, stalking, sexual exploitation, or targeted abuse.
  • Do not impersonate another person or institution.
  • Do not spam, grief, intentionally corrupt shared mission state, or exploit bugs to gain unauthorized access.
  • Keep real-world private information out of public rooms and scenario names.
  • Use “Disruptor”/hazard mechanics only inside the simulation. They are game abstractions, not instructions for real-world harm.
  • Respect classroom, museum, employer, and event rules when using institution-managed rooms.

Moderation

Production deployments should define reporting, moderation, appeal, escalation, and emergency procedures before public multiplayer or user-generated content is enabled.
