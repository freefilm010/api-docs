Methodology & Provenance

Planetary Fate Lab separates external evidence from internal simulation.

Observed facts, mission records, map/texture provenance, live operational feeds, standard physics, modeled scenarios, speculative theory, and fictional gameplay are labeled independently.

Canonical source files

data/source_registry.json contains source records. data/content_source_map.json maps each supported content item to one or more sources.

Simulation rule

Scenario telemetry deltas, game scores, fictional crew members, colony balancing, story outcomes, and versus mechanics are internal educational models unless explicitly linked to an external scientific source.

Live data

NOAA space-weather and NASA/JPL near-Earth-object feeds are labeled live only when the request succeeds. Offline or cached information must be shown as such.

Media

Planetary imagery and maps retain organization/source credits. Asset-level licensing and credit requirements take precedence over a general agency attribution.
